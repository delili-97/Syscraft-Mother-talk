Progress and leaderboard

Progress in MotherTalk is kept simple: XP for what you finish, a streak for coming back each day, and a level title that changes as XP grows. The daily goal is 30 XP, which a learner can reach with one story and a quiz point, or with two saved artworks.

XP

Story 25 XP, quiz point 10 XP, saved artwork 15 XP. Every point moves you toward the next level.

Streak

Meet the 30 XP daily goal to keep the streak alive. Miss a day, and the streak starts again.

Leaderboard

The board ranks learners by XP, so a learner can see how steady daily work adds up over time.

In the demo, the board ranks learners on the same device. It is not a shared online board from the demo alone. For the learning loop itself, see how learning works.

Daily goal

Earn 30 XP a day to keep your streak alive. A finished story gives 25 XP, a quiz point gives 10 XP, and a saved artwork gives 15 XP.
