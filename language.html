How learning works

Learning in MotherTalk follows the same short loop in every story, so a learner always knows what comes next.

  1. Choose a story from the stories page and meet its character.
  2. Read a scene and follow the story as it branches from your choices.
  3. Use Listen to hear a scene, or Practise to hear it slowly.
  4. Answer a short quiz after a scene, and check what you understood.
  5. Earn XP for the story and the quiz, and watch your streak grow.

Listen and Practise

Listen plays a scene at normal speed. Practise plays it slowly, so a learner can follow each word and repeat it. In the full app, recorded audio is the main voice. In the online demo, the device voice reads the text until the recordings are added.

Daily goal

Earn 30 XP a day to keep your streak alive. A finished story gives 25 XP, a quiz point gives 10 XP, and a saved artwork gives 15 XP.
